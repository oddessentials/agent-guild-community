# Code of conduct

The Agent Guild community is for sharing what you make and helping each other. Keep it a place people want to be.

## Do

- Be kind and assume good intent. Everyone was new once.
- Credit others when you build on their work, and follow the license they chose.
- Keep posts on topic, in the category they belong to.

## Don't

- Harass, insult or demean anyone, for any reason.
- Share anything you don't have the right to share, including other people's art, models or music.
- Post sexual, violent or hateful content, spam, or advertising.
- Try to make a Yard or piece do anything other than be one.

## When something's wrong

Use **Report content** on the post or comment. The Odd Essentials team reads every report and may edit, hide, lock
or remove posts, and block people from the community. Decisions are made case by case.

This community also follows [GitHub's Community Guidelines](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/github-terms/github-community-guidelines).
